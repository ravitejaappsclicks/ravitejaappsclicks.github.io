Data Type Description Purpose

You can request deletion of your personal information by:

  • Disconnecting Social Accounts: Revoke access through Facebook or Apple settings
  • Contacting Us: Email us to request complete data deletion

5.3 Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You can limit ad tracking and opt-out of personalized advertising:

iOS Devices:

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  2. Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  3. Or disable tracking for Tripeaks Mahjong individually

Android Devices:

  1. Open Settings → Google → Ads
  2. Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  3. Or reset your Advertising ID

5.4 Data Portability

You can request your data in a portable, machine-readable format to transfer to another service.

5.5 Managing Account Settings

  • Facebook Data: Manage in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → Tripeaks Mahjong
  • Apple Sign-In: Manage in Apple ID Settings → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID
